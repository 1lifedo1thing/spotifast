## Fixed

- **The new icon is on every screen.** 0.11.1 refreshed the app icon, but the sign-in and connecting card and Settings → About still drew the old flat disc. They now show the same icon as the app, the window and the tray, and the built-in Winamp skin's logo button and About mark have its polished look too. By @crmne.
- **No audio output no longer closes Spotifast.** On a computer without an output device, for example a new PC still missing its audio drivers, pressing play closed the app without a word. The song now pauses with a message saying no audio output device was found, in your language, and Spotifast stays open to play on another Spotify Connect device. By @crmne; thanks @spencernaugler7. (#623)
- **Desktop media controls keep up with the volume.** Changing the volume from your desktop's media controls on Linux could leave them showing the old level. By @rosset-nocpes. (#599)
